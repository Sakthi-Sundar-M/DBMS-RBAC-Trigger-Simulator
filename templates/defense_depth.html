Tier 1: Access check
PostgreSQL grants
The role's GRANTs are checked before the statement runs. Without the privilege the statement fails with SQLSTATE 42501 (insufficient_privilege), and no row is read or written.
Tier 2: Business firewalls
In-database PL/pgSQL triggers
BEFORE triggers check KYC, account status, open fraud alerts, anti-stacking and workflow rules against the incoming row. A RAISE EXCEPTION stops the statement and returns a named error code.
Tier 3: Atomicity and audit
Commit or roll back, then record
A failed check rolls back every change made by that statement, including balance updates a trigger had already applied. Successful changes are written to audit_log by AFTER triggers in the same transaction.

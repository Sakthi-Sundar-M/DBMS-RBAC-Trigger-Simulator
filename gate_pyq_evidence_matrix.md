## 4. Variant Decoupling & Pedagogical Structure

For each parent question, two distinct enterprise-grade variants are provided:
- **Variant 1 (FinTech & Banking Ledger)**: Adapts the mathematical relational invariant (e.g., B+ tree balancing, 2PL phase locking, referential integrity cascading, or BCNF closure) to transaction processing, ledger auditing, and SWIFT/ACH clearing.
- **Variant 2 (Healthcare & Clinical EMR)**: Adapts the invariant to electronic medical records, patient vitals monitoring, oncology registries, and pharmacy inventory.
- **Decoupled Answer Permutation**: The correct option in each variant is cyclically permuted (Shift +1 for Variant 1, Shift +2 for Variant 2) so that students cannot guess the variant answer by memorizing the parent key letter (`✓`).
